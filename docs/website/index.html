Plan Baltic trips with one map and three weather services side by side

Marine Weather combines open nautical charts with forecasts from MET Norway, SMHI, and FMI — plus storm radar, lightning, and national marine text summaries at your map centre.

  • Compare wind now and +3 / +6 / +12 h at any point
  • Official-style planning charts (Traficom) with MapLibre
  • English, Finnish, Swedish, and Norwegian in the app

Planning aid only — not a replacement for official charts, AIS, or regulations.

What the app does

Map & weather

Long-press the map to pick a point. See wind speed, direction, and gusts from three national models in one view. Switch between m/s and knots.

Storm radar & lightning

Animated rain radar and lightning strikes from open data (FMI, MET, SMHI). Scrub observations and short forecast on a timeline.

Marine text (4 countries)

Short sea-area summaries for Norway, Sweden, Finland, and Estonia at the map centre, with links to full forecasts on provider websites.

Route planning Premium

Draw a route on the chart, set boat speed, and compare weather along the track. Export GPX or PDF.

12-day wind outlook Premium

Extended daily wind comparison from MET Norway, SMHI, and FMI for long-range planning.

No account required

Free features work without signing in. Location stays on your device except when requesting public weather APIs.
